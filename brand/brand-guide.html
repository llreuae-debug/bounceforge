5. Do's & Don'ts

Brand integrity rules
DO

Maintain flat 2D geometry: Use solid fills and crisp vector strokes only.

Use Helvetica Neue Black (900): All lowercase ("bounceforge"), tracking -0.05em.

Watermark rule: Position Mono White horizontal lockup at bottom center (14% from bottom, 34% width, 60% opacity) with soft drop shadow.

Maintain high contrast: Render against #050507 near-black or video backdrops.

DON'T

NEVER append "3D" to the brand: No 3D badges, pills, suffixes, or taglines.

NEVER use 3D rendering for logos: No three.js, extruded text, bevels, or realistic lighting.

NEVER stretch or distort: Always constrain the proportions of the vector symbol and lockup.

NEVER substitute fonts: Strictly use Helvetica / Helvetica Neue Black 900.
